Reconstruct the latest index by hand

Each cell's contribution = weight × (price ÷ base price) × 100. The contributions sum to the published number.

Pushpak is a hackathon prototype, not an official statistic. Route weights are derived from the DGCA domestic city-pair passenger release; advance-purchase weights are a documented assumption. Fare quotes in this deployment are predominantly synthetic and labelled as such at row level. See /api/v1/methodology and /api/v1/provenance.
